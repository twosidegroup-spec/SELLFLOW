Privacy

Plainly written, because a privacy policy nobody can read is not a privacy policy.

What we store

{{name}} stores the business information you enter: your products and stock levels, orders, customer names and contact details, courier and tracking information, payments, expenses and your account credentials. That is the data needed to run your business, and {{name}} does not collect anything else.

Where it is kept

Your records are stored in a hosted PostgreSQL database. Every query is scoped to your own business, and that isolation is enforced in the database with row-level security rather than only in the app.

Your account credentials

Passwords are hashed before they are stored and are never recoverable in plain text. The mobile application ships with only a public, read-restricted API key; privileged access stays on the server and is never included in the app.

On your device

The app caches data locally so it can work through a lost connection, and queues changes until they can be sent. Clearing the app or uninstalling it removes that local cache. Cached data stays on your device.

Third parties

{{name}} does not sell your data and does not share it for advertising. If courier status information is shared with a courier to track a parcel, it is only what that courier needs to identify and move your shipment.

Deletion

You can archive and delete products and customers from inside the app. To request deletion of your account and its records, contact {{supportEmail}}.

Reviews on this website

The review form on this site has no public database connected to it. A review you submit is stored in your own browser only and is not published or shared. No review is ever displayed here unless it has been submitted and moderated.
